Android runs a shell directly on your device, with no root filesystem download and no proot layer.

It opens instantly in the built-in terminal, using the app's own sandbox permissions. Busybox is placed on the PATH so familiar GNU-style tools (grep, tar, find, vi, wget, sed, awk) work alongside Android's own toybox utilities.

If the device is rooted, the shell automatically starts as root; otherwise it runs as the app user.
